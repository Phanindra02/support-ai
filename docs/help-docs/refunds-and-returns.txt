Brightleaf Home: Refunds and Returns

Our return window
You can return most items within 30 days of delivery. Items must be unused and in their original packaging. Sale items marked "Final sale" can't be returned.

How to start a return
Log in to your account, open Orders, choose the order, and select "Return an item". We email you a prepaid return label within one business day. Print it, attach it to the box, and drop the box at any UPS location.

Return shipping costs
Returns are free when the item arrived damaged or we sent the wrong item. For any other reason, we take a $6.95 return shipping fee out of your refund.

When you get your money back
Once your return reaches our warehouse, we inspect it within 2 business days. After that, your refund takes 5 to 7 business days to show up. Refunds always go back to the original payment method. If you paid with a gift card, the refund goes back onto a new gift card.

Damaged or wrong items
If an item arrives damaged, email a photo of the damage to support@brightleaf.example within 7 days of delivery. We send a free replacement, or a full refund if you prefer, and you don't need to send the damaged item back.

Exchanges
We don't do direct exchanges. Return the item for a refund, then place a new order for the item you want.

Large furniture
Sofas, beds, and tables over 50 pounds are collected from your home by our delivery partner. Call us to book a pickup. The pickup fee is $49, and it's waived if the item was damaged.
