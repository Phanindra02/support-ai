Brightleaf Home: Shipping and Delivery

Where we ship
We ship to all 50 US states and to Canada. We don't ship to other countries yet, and we can't deliver to PO boxes for furniture orders.

Shipping options and costs
Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days and costs $5.99. It's free on orders over $75. Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days and costs $14.99. Orders to Canada take 7 to 10 business days and cost $19.99, and the customer pays any import duties.

When your order ships
Orders placed before 2 p.m. Eastern Time on a business day ship the same day. Orders placed later, or on weekends, ship the next business day.

Tracking your order
When your order ships, we email you a tracking number. You can also find it under Orders in your account. Tracking can take up to 24 hours to show the first update.

Changing your address
You can change the delivery address for 1 hour after you place the order, under Orders in your account. After that the order is being packed, so contact us and we'll try to redirect it with the carrier.

Lost or late packages
If tracking hasn't changed for 5 business days, or the package says delivered but you can't find it, contact us. We'll open a claim with the carrier and send a replacement once the claim is filed, usually within 3 business days.

Furniture delivery
Furniture comes by our delivery partner, who calls you to book a 4-hour delivery window. White glove delivery, which includes carrying the item to the room you choose and setting it up, costs $99.
