Brightleaf Home: Your Account and Payments

Payment methods
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, Apple Pay, and Brightleaf gift cards. We don't accept cash on delivery or checks.

When you're charged
Your card is authorized when you place the order, and charged when the order ships. If an order ships in parts, each part is charged when it ships.

Paying in installments
Orders over $150 can be paid in 4 interest-free installments through Klarna. Choose Klarna at checkout. The first payment is taken at checkout, and the rest every 2 weeks.

Gift cards
Gift cards come in amounts from $25 to $500 and never expire. You can check a gift card's balance at brightleaf.example/giftcard. A gift card can't be exchanged for cash.

Resetting your password
Select "Forgot password" on the login page and enter your email address. We send a reset link that works for 30 minutes. If it doesn't arrive, check your spam folder.

Changing your email address
Go to Account settings, select "Email", and enter the new address. We send a confirmation link to the new address, and the change takes effect once you open it.

Closing your account
Email support@brightleaf.example from the address on your account and ask us to close it. We delete your personal data within 30 days, except order records, which we must keep for 7 years for tax purposes.

Price adjustments
If an item you bought drops in price within 14 days of your order, contact us and we refund the difference. This doesn't apply to flash sales.
